id-scan-and-liveness-check-with-face-comparing demo

Combines id-ocr-web and liveness-check-web with a face-comparison step in between: pick an already cropped/edge-straightened ID image, then run the liveness challenge sequence against your camera. When it finishes, a face descriptor is pulled from the ID photo and from your live camera frame and compared - all in the browser. See ../README.md for how to wire verifyIdentity into a real app.
